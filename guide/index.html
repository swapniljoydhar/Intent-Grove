A FIELD GUIDE

Notice the path.
Keep the choice.

Intent Grove gives you a private, visual record of how a browsing session unfolds. You set the intention; the extension can offer a gentle prompt; you decide what the path means and what to do next.

A reflection tool, not a psychological test. The tree is a metaphor for recorded navigation. It does not assess personality, attention, mood, health, motivation, or whether a page was useful.

01 · BEGIN

Start with a reference point

Choose what matters for this session

Write a short intention in your own words. It is a reference point for later reflection, not a rule you have to follow. You can add a private note about why it matters and choose what you might do if a reminder appears.

When you start, your intention is also sent as a search query to the provider selected in Settings. The optional personal note stays on this device and is not sent to the search provider.

Or browse without one

Choose Explore Without Intention whenever that feels right. Intent Grove does not block websites, close tabs, or require you to return to a particular page.

You can change your intention, pause the companion, or end a session at any time.

02 · THE GARDEN MAP

What the tree shows

The tree turns browser-observed page changes into a navigation path you can inspect. It describes the route’s shape, not meaning or relevance; it does not decide whether a page was useful or aligned with your intention.

Read it from the root outward

Root
The page where the session began.
Branch
A recorded move to another page or route.
Leaf
A page at the end of a path recorded so far.
Depth
The number of recorded navigation steps from the root to a page.

Links and some tab or route changes can be connected; other pages may be marked as unlinked when their relationship is unclear. A longer path is not automatically a detour.

03 · A MOMENT TO CHOOSE

The choice card is an invitation

At the reminder point you chose in Settings, an on-page card can describe the recorded path and offer a pause. It does not cover your page with a blocker or decide which option is best.

Your settings shape the timing

The quiet reminder and choice point are adjustable. You can pause the companion on one site or pause it everywhere. Reminder wording is a prompt for your own reflection, not a verdict about the page.

Elapsed time is only elapsed time

Session time can include time away from the browser, and tab time does not measure attention or active reading. Treat these numbers as rough records, not productivity scores.

04 · OPTIONAL REFLECTION

Weekly path patterns

Insights & Stats can show counts of recorded forks, straight-through paths, explicit search refinements, and return events from gardens still in local history. One garden can contribute to more than one pattern. These are descriptions of browser events—not behavioral archetypes, personality labels, or evidence that a path did or did not serve you.

The weekly reminder is off by default. If enabled, its quiet note appears only inside Insights & Stats, at most once every seven days, after enough completed gardens are available. It does not use browser or operating-system notifications.

05 · YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

What stays here—and what does not

Stored on this device

Garden history, recorded page paths, your optional personal note, and your settings are kept in the browser’s local extension storage. Intent Grove does not send garden history to its developers and has no extension telemetry.

You can review, export, import, or clear local data from the Garden Map.

Sent to your chosen search provider

When you plant an intention, the text is also used as a search query and is sent to your selected search provider. Your optional personal note is not included in that query.

QUICK ANSWERS

A few things you may be wondering

Does the tree tell me whether I was focused?

No. It shows recorded navigation relationships and rough elapsed time. It cannot observe your thoughts, attention, or whether a page was useful.

Is this a psychological or clinical test?

No. It is a browsing-reflection tool, not a validated psychological assessment, diagnosis, or treatment tool.

Will a reminder stop me from opening a page?

No. The web stays open. You can keep exploring, return, save the page, or choose another intention.

Does this demo touch my real browsing history?

No. It uses only the fictional sample shown here. Its buttons change explanatory text on this page and nothing else.

Use the parts that help; ignore the rest. Your choices remain yours.
